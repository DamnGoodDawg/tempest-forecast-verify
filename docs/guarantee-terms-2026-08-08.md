## Key clauses for the claim

- Applies to Tempest Weather Systems for the **8 months immediately following the date of
  purchase** (station purchased late May 2026 → claim deadline ~late Jan 2027, matching the
  tracker's milestones).
- Qualification: properly installed at a single fixed location, **continuous reporting for at
  least 5 months**, damage-free and fully functional the whole period.
- The test: forecast "not measurably superior to forecasts publicly available elsewhere for
  the **3-month period immediately preceding your request**" — the rolling-90-day window the
  dashboard's headline verdict now uses.
- Claimant must provide "a statement that includes enough information for us to reasonably
  ascertain that the Tempest Weather System did not provide a better forecast" — the
  scoreboard + verdict history is that statement.
- Refund is full purchase price; return shipping at the customer's expense.

Refund and Return Policy.

We believe that you will be satisfied with WeatherFlow's products, but if the products are not
what you expected them to be, WeatherFlow offers the following three return and refund

Unconditional 60-Day Exchange Policy.

If you received a defective or damaged item, please contact us within 60 days of your date of

Unconditional 60-Day Money Back Return Policy.

All of WeatherFlow's non-sale products come with a 60-day money back guarantee from the date

damaged in any way. Please contact us to request a return. We will ask you to return the
product to us and we will process your refund based upon the original payment method once we

Better Forecast Guarantee.

Our Better Forecast Guarantee is a return policy which applies only to Tempest Weather Systems
for the 8 months immediately following the date of purchase. WeatherFlow guarantees the
Tempest Weather System will produce a better forecast for the specific location in which it is
installed. To qualify, you must properly install your Tempest System at a single fixed
location and consistently measure and report data for a continuous period of at least 5
months. Your Tempest Weather System must be free of damage and fully functional for the entire
5-month period (although reasonable signs of use are acceptable). If you believe the forecast
provided by your Tempest Weather System is not measurably superior to forecasts publicly
available elsewhere for the 3-month period immediately preceding your request, you may request
a full refund for the Tempest Weather System, so long as such request is submitted no later
than 8 months after the date of purchase. Please contact us to request a return under the
Better Forecast Guarantee. You must provide us with a statement that includes enough
information for us to reasonably ascertain that the Tempest Weather System did not provide a
better forecast. We may require you to return the Tempest Weather System at your expense. We
will ask you to return the product to us and we will process your refund based upon the
original payment method once we receive and inspect the returned product. You are responsible
for paying shipping costs to return the product to WeatherFlow.
